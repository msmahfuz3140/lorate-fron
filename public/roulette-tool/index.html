STRATEGIES
n9 = the number + the 4 pockets on each side = 9 pockets. There is only n9 — every prediction is two numbers, 9 pockets each = exactly 18 pockets, e.g. 26n9 32n9. The two centres are always at least 9 pockets apart on the wheel, so the two arcs never overlap — that is what keeps the bet at exactly 18 distinct numbers instead of collapsing into one arc. PRIV 1 = n9 around the last spin + n9 far away. PRIV 2 = n9 around the opposite of the last spin + n9 far away. PRIV 3 = n9 on the last spin + n9 on its opposite (board mirror, e.g. 14↔6). MARKOV (Strategy 8) = 1st & 2nd order pocket-to-sector Markov transition probability + wheel step flow. A win = the ball lands inside the 18 covered pockets.
